Paceria tracks reading and movement at your own pace.

It does not ask you to show up every day. It shows the small wins that a period reveals: you moved twice this week, you finished a book this month.

■ No daily streak to break
Paceria never counts consecutive days. Taking a day off breaks nothing. The only question it asks is whether you met the amount you set, within the period you chose.

■ Reading and movement, side by side
Mind and body, kept in view together. By tracking only these two, Paceria stays legible in a way general-purpose habit trackers cannot.

■ You decide what enough looks like
One book a month, two sessions a week — or whatever fits. Goals can change at any time. Lowering your target during a busy month is an adjustment, not a failure.

■ Continuity you can see as fact
"6 of 8 weeks on pace." Plain facts, stated plainly. No scores, no rankings, no black-box index.

■ Logging takes seconds
Pick a movement type and save. Add a book with just its title. If logging is a chore, it will not last.

■ Everything stays on your device
No account. No network requests. Your records never leave your phone.
